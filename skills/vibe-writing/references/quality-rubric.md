# Quality Rubric

Run hard gates before evaluating style. Apply the remaining dimensions in light
of the actual task, genre, author, and audience; do not force one house style.

## Hard Gates

Treat any failure below as blocking for the affected passage:

| Gate | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| Fabrication | No invented data, quotations, cases, achievements, sources, or first-person experience. |
| Plan versus result | Proposed, attempted, and completed work are clearly distinguished. |
| Sensitive material | Protected personal or project material is not exposed or reused outside its allowed boundary. |
| Low-confidence inference | Tentative voice traits and unverified claims are not presented as confirmed. |
| Task conflict | The output follows the current instruction, requested mode, scope, and register. |
| Forced personality | Emotion, humor, slang, roughness, and anecdotes appear only when supported by the author or task. |

Remove, correct, qualify, or explicitly flag the failing material before
delivery. Do not weaken the gate merely to make a draft sound complete.

## Quality Dimensions

Evaluate the dimensions relevant to the task:

- **Factual reliability:** Claims have the right evidence and uncertainty.
- **Voice fidelity:** The prose follows confirmed project evidence without
  copying phrases or exaggerating a persona.
- **Clarity:** Readers can follow the thesis, references, terminology, and
  reasoning without unnecessary friction.
- **Reader value:** The content provides useful information, judgment, or action
  for its intended audience.
- **Structure and rhythm:** Organization, transitions, sentence variation, and
  density serve the content and platform.
- **Boundary awareness:** The draft distinguishes fact, inference, opinion,
  experience, and protected material.
- **Task completion:** The requested artifact, scope, format, and any required
  notes or change summary are present.

Use qualitative judgment rather than a fixed numeric score. Fix the highest
impact problem first and preserve deliberate choices that serve the author.

## Formulaic AI-Sounding Prose

Treat formulaic AI-sounding prose as a contextual symptom, never as a
forbidden-word or detector-score test. Look for patterns such as empty setup,
unsupported generalization, repetitive structure, over-balanced phrasing,
generic transitions, or a register that conflicts with the author and task.

A word, phrase, sentence length, polished paragraph, or rhetorical pattern is
not a defect by itself. Revise only when the pattern harms truth, voice, clarity,
reader value, rhythm, boundaries, or task completion. Never promise or optimize
for an AI-detector score.
