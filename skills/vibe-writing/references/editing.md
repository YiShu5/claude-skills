# Editing

Choose the mode from the user's requested degree of change. Do not silently turn
a review into a rewrite or a focused edit into a new article.

## Modes

| Mode | Contract |
|---|---|
| Edit | Preserve meaning and voice while fixing the requested problems. |
| Rewrite | Allow structural and stylistic change within the stated goal and factual boundaries. |
| Review | Diagnose problems and recommend changes without rewriting unless requested. |

If the request is ambiguous between edit and rewrite, prefer the less invasive
edit. Ask only when the difference would materially change the result.

## Protect the Author's Meaning and Voice

Before changing text, identify its thesis, stance, factual status, uncertainty,
progression, and deliberate texture. Preserve:

- Qualifications such as “可能”, “我不确定”, and scoped conclusions.
- Stance and degree of commitment unless the user asks to change them.
- Hesitation, repetition, fragments, unusual rhythm, or deliberate roughness
  when they carry voice or meaning.
- Personal and factual boundaries from the source text.

Do not make a claim sound more certain, polished, emotional, or authoritative
than its evidence supports. Do not normalize every sentence into a single
rhythm or substitute generic fluency for the author's choices.

## Execute the Mode

For an edit, change only what is needed to meet the request. For a rewrite,
change structure and style as needed while preserving any meanings and facts the
goal does not authorize changing. For a review, return each material issue as:

1. Problem.
2. Effect on the reader or task.
3. Recommendation.

Quote only the minimum excerpt needed to locate an issue. Do not supply a full
replacement passage in review mode unless requested.

Use fact checking when revisions touch personal claims, time-sensitive facts,
or unsupported assertions. Use the quality rubric without erasing intentional
voice features.

## Delivery

For edits and rewrites, deliver:

1. The revised text.
2. A brief change summary naming the important changes and what was preserved.

For reviews, deliver the diagnosis and recommendations only, plus factual notes
when requested or necessary. Do not claim to have revised text that was only
reviewed.
